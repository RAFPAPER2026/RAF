Title: Advances in Computer Vision (Version 18)
Authors: David Black; Eva Green; Frank Brown
Year: 2021
Keywords: computer vision; image recognition; convolutional networks

Abstract:
Recent advances in computer vision have been driven by
convolutional neural networks. This survey summarizes key
developments.

References:
Y. LeCun, Deep Learning, 2015.
I. Goodfellow, GANs, 2014.
